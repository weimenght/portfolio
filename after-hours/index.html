After hours

People aren't the means.
People are the point.

After work I make small, free things that try to take that seriously: a town, a jar, a way to stay close, a few words in someone else's language. None of them want more of your time. They'd rather you spent it with someone.

🏘️ Nearby🫙 Little Things💌 Handoff🗣️ Vocab
How I design them

Five rules I keep

  1. 🌿It ends off the screen.Every visit closes with something to do away from it: call someone, cook, go outside, sleep.
  2. 🎈Nothing to win.No scores, streaks or badges. Nothing counts how long you stayed.
  3. 👥Made for two.Most things work alone or with someone far away, from one link.
  4. 🔒Yours stays yours.No ads, no tracking, sign-in only where it has to be. Most of it never leaves your device.
  5. ☕Small on purpose.One thing at a time. A short visit is a good visit.
