HOW TO PLAY

HIT THE NOTESRecords slide down four lanes. Hit each one as it crosses the line: D F J K, the arrow keys, or tap the lane buttons. You play the melody, so a miss leaves a hole in the song.
HOLD & SCRATCHLong notes: keep the key down until the tail passes. Striped bars across all lanes: SCRATCH with Space or the vinyl button.
GOLD NOTESGold records fire the airhorn and pay double.
KEEP THE CROWDMisses drain the CROWD meter. Empty it and everyone goes home. Combos raise your multiplier up to x4.
DROP THE BASSFill HYPE, then press DROP (Enter) for double points while the lasers go wild.
FREE JAMEight pads on keys 1–4 and Q–R, a scratchable vinyl, and a two-bar loop recorder. Some riffs are worth discovering.

SETTINGS

If your hits feel early, move the offset left. If they feel late (common with Bluetooth headphones), move it right.
